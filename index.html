Oxfordshire was hotter than the Sun

The facts behind it
  • The UK's hottest day: 40.3 °C at Coningsby, 19 July 2022 (Met Office).
  • JET, a fusion experiment in Oxfordshire, held plasma at 150 million °C. It closed in December 2023.
  • The Sun's core is about 15 million °C, so JET ran ten times hotter.
  • The record is CERN's: over 5 trillion °C (estimated), lasting about 10⁻²³ seconds.
  • UKAEA: the JET experiment↗
  • CERN: heavy ions and quark-gluon plasma↗
  • NASA: the Sun↗

You're Faster Than Any Plane Right Now

The facts behind it
  • A cheetah: 58 mph, the fastest measured by tracking collar (Nature, 2013).
  • The X-15 rocket plane: 4,520 mph in 1967, still the fastest crewed plane.
  • Parker Solar Probe: 430,000 mph, the fastest spacecraft ever (2024).
  • You: about 67,000 mph around the Sun, right now.
  • Smithsonian: the X-15↗
  • NASA: Parker Solar Probe↗
  • National Geographic: cheetah collars↗

Something in Your Kitchen Is 13.8 Billion Years Old

The facts behind it
  • The oldest bread found: crumbs about 14,400 years old, from Jordan, before farming began.
  • Jonathan the tortoise hatched around 1832 and is still alive on St Helena.
  • A Greenland shark was estimated at 392 years old (Science, 2016).
  • Almost all the hydrogen in your body was made minutes after the Big Bang. So the oldest thing in your kitchen is you.
  • PBS NOVA: the Greenland shark↗

Your Eyes Can See Light Older Than Our Species

The facts behind it
  • The Andromeda galaxy, about 2.5 million light years away, can be seen without a telescope from a dark site.
  • Its light set off long before our species appeared, about 300,000 years ago.
  • Voyager 1 is predicted to reach one light-day from Earth on 18 November 2026.
  • Webb's record galaxy, MoM-z14: its light travelled about 13.5 billion years.
  • NASA: Voyager↗

How Could a T. Rex Lose to an Insect?

The facts behind it
  • T. rex's bite: about 35,000 newtons, estimated from jaw models.
  • The strongest bite ever actually measured: a saltwater crocodile, 16,414 N (2012).
  • The Tsavo lions were said to have killed 135 people; tests on their bones suggest about 35.
  • Mosquitoes: malaria killed an estimated 610,000 people in 2024 (WHO). The death rate has more than halved since 2000.
  • WHO: malaria fact sheet↗
  • Field Museum: the Tsavo lions↗
  • Florida Museum: shark attack data↗

Can GTA 6 Beat the Best-Selling Games of All Time?

The facts behind it
  • GTA V: over 230 million sold (Take-Two, August 2026).
  • The PlayStation 2 sold over 160 million, the best-selling console ever (Sony).
  • Wii Sports: 82.9 million, the most for any game on one console (Nintendo).
  • The Atari 2600 had 128 bytes of memory, less than one text message.

How we check

Why you can trust a number here

Two sources

Every figure needs two independent sources, ideally the people who measured it: NASA, the Met Office, CERN, museums and journals.

A second pair of eyes

Each book and video gets a separate fact audit whose only job is to find mistakes.

Honest labels

We say whether a number was measured, estimated or argued about, and give records a date, because records get broken.
