No made-up facts

How we check

The internet is full of “facts” that aren't. Here's what every number goes through before it goes in a book or a video.

Two sources

Every figure needs two independent sources, ideally the people who measured it: NASA, the Met Office, CERN, museums and journals.

A fact hunt

Each book and video then gets a separate audit whose only job is to find mistakes. It always finds some, and we fix them.

Honest labels

We say whether a number was measured, estimated or argued about, and we date records, because records get broken.
